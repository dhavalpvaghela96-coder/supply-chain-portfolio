## What This Project Is About

After COVID, demand in Canadian manufacturing came back fast but supply chains couldn't keep up. I used 10 years of Statistics Canada data to understand what actually happened and what it means for production planning today.

---

## Project Details

| | |
|---|---|
| **Data Source** | Statistics Canada, Table 16-10-0047-01 |
| **Industry** | Fabricated Metal Product Manufacturing, NAICS 332 |
| **Date Range** | January 2015 to December 2025 |
| **Dataset Size** | 1,188 rows, 10 metrics per month |
| **Tools Used** | Excel, Power Query, Pivot Tables |
| **Methodology** | Time-series trend analysis, YoY growth, unfilled-to-new-orders ratio |

---

## Key Finding

After COVID, new orders in Canadian fabricated metal manufacturing jumped 23% in one year. By 2022, unfilled orders were $1 billion above pre-COVID levels and stayed there through 2023. This was not a company problem, the entire supply chain was overwhelmed. Any production planner still using pre-2020 numbers would be under-ordering materials without even knowing it.
